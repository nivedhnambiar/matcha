Chapter oneThe idea

It began with a small observation. Lip balm is the ritual we reach for most often, in bags, in pockets, in the quiet moments between things, and yet it is rarely designed with the care we give the rest of our routine.

Chapter twoThe flavours

We chose to make one thing and give it our full attention. Each flavour starts as a memory, a morning coffee, a ripe banana, a bowl of summer cherries, and is translated patiently into a balm that feels soft, glossy and weightless.

Chapter threeThe promise

Everything we make is vegan. Nothing is ever tested on animals. And because lips are as individual as the people who wear them, every formula is gentle enough for every skin, in every season.

Small things, made with great care, are the ones people keep.

Vegan by design

Plant-derived waxes and butters. No beeswax, no lanolin, no compromise on comfort.

Never tested on animals

Cruelty free at every stage, from the first formula to the finished tube.

Made for every skin

A gentle, lightly fragranced formula suited to all skin types and tones.

From fruit to balm.

  1. ChooseWe begin with a single fruit, flower or flavour and ask how it should feel on the lips.
  2. InfuseSoft plant butters are gently infused so the flavour stays true and never sharp.
  3. WhipThe blend is worked until it is smooth, glossy and light to wear.
  4. FinishEach balm is tinted, comfort tested, poured and sealed in its signature tube.
